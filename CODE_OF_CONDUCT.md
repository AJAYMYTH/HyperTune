# 🤝 Code of Conduct — HyperTune

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers of **HyperTune** pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

---

## Our Standards

### ✅ Examples of Behavior That Contributes to a Positive Environment

- Being respectful and empathetic toward other community members
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Gracefully accepting constructive feedback and criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community and device safety
- Sharing verified, evidence-based optimization advice
- Helping newcomers understand the framework and safety guardrails
- Giving credit to original authors and contributors

### ❌ Examples of Unacceptable Behavior

- Trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments
- Personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment of any kind
- Publishing others' private information (doxing), including device identifiers (IMEI, serial numbers, phone numbers)
- Recommending or sharing dangerous ADB commands that could brick or bootloop devices
- Deliberately bypassing safety guardrails or encouraging others to do so
- Sharing unverified "debloating" lists that risk system stability
- Sexual language, imagery, or unwelcome advances
- Any conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

---

## 🛡️ Device Safety Clause

> [!IMPORTANT]
> HyperTune exists to **safely** optimize Xiaomi devices. Any community member who knowingly shares commands, scripts, or instructions that could cause bootloops, data loss, or system instability will face immediate enforcement action.

This includes:
- Sharing bulk `pm uninstall` or `pm disable` commands targeting blacklisted packages
- Encouraging root modifications without proper disclaimers and rollback instructions
- Posting unverified optimization claims as facts

---

## 📋 Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, including:

- GitHub repository (issues, PRs, discussions, wiki)
- Commit messages and code comments
- Community chat channels (Discord, Telegram, etc.)
- Social media interactions when representing the project
- In-person or virtual events related to the project

It also applies when an individual is officially representing the community in public spaces.

## 🚨 Enforcement

### Reporting

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers:

- **Email:** [contact.javaliajayakumar@gmail.com](mailto:contact.javaliajayakumar@gmail.com)
- **GitHub:** Open a private report via [GitHub's reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter.

### Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers will follow these Community Impact Guidelines to determine consequences:

#### 1. 📝 Correction
**Community Impact:** Use of inappropriate language or minor unprofessional behavior.

**Consequence:** A private written warning, providing clarity around the nature of the violation and an explanation of why the behavior was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.

#### 2. ⚠️ Warning
**Community Impact:** A violation through a single incident or series of actions.

**Consequence:** A formal warning with consequences for continued behavior. No interaction with the people involved for a specified period. Violating these terms may lead to a temporary or permanent ban.

#### 3. 🚫 Temporary Ban
**Community Impact:** A serious violation of community standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior or sharing dangerous device commands.

**Consequence:** A temporary ban from all community interaction and public communication for a specified period. No public or private interaction with the people involved is allowed. Violating these terms may lead to a permanent ban.

#### 4. 🔴 Permanent Ban
**Community Impact:** Demonstrating a pattern of violation, sustained harassment, aggression, or deliberately sharing device-bricking commands.

**Consequence:** A permanent ban from all community interaction.
